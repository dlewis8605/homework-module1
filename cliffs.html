California's Greatest Cliffs

The greatest cliffs are these four cliffs located in southern California. Each of these cliffs have their own uniqueness and beauty: West Cliff, North Cliff, East Cliff and South Cliff are the pride of California. Each cliff has many amenities to offer visitors such as recreational areas, camping sites, wildlife watch spots and overnight cabins.

No matter which direction you head from Irvine, California, the sites are easily reached within a 100 mile radius. West Cliff is the most popular among Westcliff University campus students because of its close proximity.

West Cliff

Experience the ultimate coastal trek at West Cliff! Hike along stunning, sun-drenched trails offering breathtaking panoramic views of the vibrant, emerald-blue sea. Discover hidden coves, marvel at dramatic, jagged rock formations rising directly from the ocean, and watch distant boats glide by as you conquer this perfect seaside adventure.

North Cliff

Elevate your next alpine adventure at North Cliff! A hiker’s dream, these rugged trails wind through dense, lush green forests before opening up to spectacular, jagged rocky outcroppings. Standing on the sheer precipice under endless blue skies, you'll be rewarded with breathtaking, sweeping vistas over miles of pristine, undulating mountain ranges.

East Cliff

Prepare for an awe-inspiring, bucket-list journey to East Cliff! This iconic, heart-pounding ascent leads you to a dramatic, sheer flat-topped precipice towering hundreds of feet over a majestic, deep-blue fjord. Join fellow adventurers at the legendary summit where the unbelievable, world-class panoramic views will leave you completely speechless.

South Cliff

Discover the rugged, untamed beauty of South Cliff! Traverse winding, sun-baked desert trails surrounded by striking red and orange sandstone walls, vibrant canyon brush, and a towering central rock monolith. This magnificent canyon offers fiery, unforgettable landscapes and an ancient, otherworldly atmosphere that will thrill any trailblazer looking for raw wilderness.
